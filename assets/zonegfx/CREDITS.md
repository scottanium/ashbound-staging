# zonegfx art credits / provenance

All zone art under `assets/zonegfx/` was made for Ashbound in Nov 2026; no third-party textures or stock assets are used.

- **Painted textures and sprites** (`<set>/*.webp` listed under `sets.<set>.import` in `data/zonegfx.json`): generated with Grok Bot's image generator from text prompts written for this project (style reference: `world-refs/src/act2-*.jpg`), then processed by `tools/import_zonegfx.py` (seamless tiling, magenta key, despill, trim, resize). Source images are kept outside the repo in `/workspace/ashbound-gfx/zonegfx-notes/gen-src/`.
- **Procedural maps and decals** (`detail`, `ripple`, puddle/scum/mudsplat/stones/roots/planks/bones): generated by `tools/gen_zonegfx.py` from the recipes in `sets.<set>.gen` (numpy noise; original work, CC0).
- **v84r Act 1 sets** (`act1` shared interactables kit, `wood`, `salt`, `spire`, `kiln`, `town`): same process and provenance; source images in `/workspace/ashbound-gfx/zonegfx-notes/act1/gen-src/<set>/`, set definitions generated by `tools/act1_sets.py`.
- **v84r Act 2 sets** (`cave`, `archive`, `flats`, `brine`): same process; they borrow most art from the Act 1 sets through `use{}` and import only a few new pieces from `/workspace/ashbound-gfx/zonegfx-notes/act2/gen-src/<set>/`; definitions generated by `tools/act2_sets.py`.
- **v84t Act 3 sets** (`orchard`, `orchtown`, `nave`, `hush`): same process; new floor/roots textures and tree, briar, barrel, pew and empty-frame sprites from `/workspace/ashbound-gfx/zonegfx-notes/act3/gen-src/<set>/`, the rest borrowed from the Act 1/2 sets through `use{}`; definitions generated by `tools/act3_sets.py`.
- **v84s Act 2 fixes** (`pool`, `flats`): `pool` (Queen Pool) imports two painted floor textures (`poolfloor`, `poolrubble`) and borrows the rest from `cave`; `flats` imports two opaque obsidian-mirror crystal sprites (`mirror2`, `mirrorcluster`) replacing the translucent ones. Same process; sources in `/workspace/ashbound-gfx/zonegfx-notes/act2/gen-src/{pool,flats}/`; definitions in `tools/act2_sets.py`.
